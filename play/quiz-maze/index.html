Enemies move like chess pieces, one step for each move you make: 👁️ a hunter picks up your trail and closes in (you can see it even through fog), and 👻 shades wander. A correct answer's dash carries you several cells for one of their steps, so it's how you pull away; fast travel gives them a step every 3 cells. Getting caught costs a ❤️, and the hunter then loses your trail for 10–12 moves. Z zaps the nearest enemy in a straight line (marked ⚡ when one is in range): answer right to banish it for a while.
A correct answer lets you dash down the corridor to the next junction. A wrong answer costs a ❤️ and knocks you back up to 3 cells. Walking through cells you've already visited is free, and F fast-travels to the nearest unexplored opening. Stopping on a junction with full hearts saves a 🚩 checkpoint. Lose all 3 ❤️ and you decohere: back to that checkpoint, and ground explored since then must be re-earned (you keep the map). Every 3-answer streak restores a ❤️, or at full hearts earns a 50/50 token (max 3).
Each maze has 3 🎯 bonus objectives worth +30 XP. Pick up coins for XP, and look in dead ends for power-ups: ❤️ heart, 🛡️ shield, ✂️ 50/50 token, 🔦 lantern, ⚡ double XP. Dead ends can also hide 📜 physicist cards for your Codex.
Adventure mode: 🗿 🔒 🧩 👹 encounters guard items (answer a question or roll the dice; each failed attempt adds +2 to later dice rolls there), and the 🐉 exit is the boss's lair, where the boss grows stronger every 3 rounds. Once you hold the 🔮 bane, a glowing trail leads to the lair. Your hero trains as you play: XP you earn in adventure mode raises your hero's level, and each level gives +1 Might, Guard and Wit and +2 max HP.
