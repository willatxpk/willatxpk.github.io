    How it works
    
      
      
    • BibTeX comes from doi.org (falling back to Crossref). arXiv IDs are looked up through their arXiv DOI. Nothing else leaves your browser.
      • 
      
    • Your library is saved in this browser. In Chrome or Edge you can also Open a .bib file on your computer and Fetcher will save straight into it.
      • 
      
    • Papers already in the library (same DOI or arXiv ID) are not added twice. If a citation key is already taken, the new one gets a letter: Smith_2020b.
      • 
      
    • Format & check: pick a journal to load its rules (author cut-off, abbreviated journal names). Entries that would print with gaps, such as no page or article number, get a ⚠ flag. Fill missing info looks those up on Crossref. Your library keeps the full data; the formatted .bib and reference list are made fresh for each journal.
      • 
      
    • doi.org often leaves out article numbers (PRL, Nature Communications, …), so Fetcher fills them in from Crossref when you fetch.
      • 
      
    • Shortcuts: Enter fetch, Shift+Enter new line, / filter, Esc clear.
      • 
    
    
  
    
  
    Your bibliography stays on your device.
